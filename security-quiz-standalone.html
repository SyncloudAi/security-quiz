Security Guidelines & Best Practices

We're a small team (~12 people) handling sensitive infrastructure data for our customers. This isn't a compliance formality — it's the reason customers trust us with read access to their cloud environments.

Read the full briefing

1. Phishing

  • Check the sender address carefully, not just the display name.
  • Never enter your password after clicking a link in an email — go to the site directly instead.
  • If something feels off, it probably is. Report it in #incident-response — even if you're not sure.

2. MFA & Passwords

  • MFA is required on every in-scope company application — not optional.
  • Never share your login with a coworker, even briefly — they get their own account instead.
  • Use a unique password per service.

3. Devices

  • Company laptops must have disk encryption and up-to-date anti-malware protection active.
  • Only install software through the approved process, not random downloads.
  • If your device is lost or stolen, tell us immediately so it can be remotely wiped.

4. Clear Desk & Screen Lock

  • Lock your screen every time you step away — office, café, airport, doesn't matter.
  • Don't leave sensitive material visible to visitors or in public spaces.
  • Visitors are always logged, identified, and escorted — no exceptions.

5. Incidents & Reporting

  • Report anything that might be a security issue in #incident-response right away.
  • You don't need to be certain it's real — reporting a false alarm costs five minutes.
  • Speed is the whole point: the faster something is reported, the smaller the impact.
